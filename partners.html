Requirements

What you need to get started

We maintain high standards to protect our carriers, our shippers, and the freight we move. Here's what you'll need:

  • Active MC Authority (FMCSA)

    Current Motor Carrier authority in good standing with the FMCSA.

  • Good safety record

    Satisfactory DOT safety rating and a clean compliance history.

  • Valid insurance coverage

    Active Auto Liability ($1M) and Cargo ($100k) policies.

  • Signed carrier agreement

    Completed and executed GFS GROUP LLC carrier packet.
